WHAT THIS IS. The robot fighters from the spectator page, animated. Idle breathes at 5 frames a second with a blink. Jab, kick and the hit reaction run at 12; block, duck and throw at 10; the bow and recover at 6; exhausted at 5; the salute and the fall at 8. Frame zero of idle is the static sprite byte for byte.
