## Mechanical correctness (hard gate): passed

- Every hand vector in [combat.md](combat.md) §8, in Python, C++ and the
  browser engine.
- 100,000 generated fights agree with the independent reference aid
  (`QDOJO_COMBAT_CASES=100000`), plus 42,336 paired boundary beats with slot
  symmetry.
- 10,000 frozen full fights replay identically in Python,
  `contracts/combat_core` and `apps/web/combat/engine.js`, including per-beat
  detail and reason codes for 200 traced fights.
- The frozen commitment fixture and NIST SHA-256 vectors reproduce.
- The contract reference and its bounded C++ port end on identical event
  digests over four input journals, which emit all 29 event types.
  Conservation is asserted after every step.

How this was reached, including the failures:

1. **First campaign: 6 of 10 gates passed.** It reported one uncountered
   strategy (J,K cycle), two ablations below 0.05, and only two strong
   families.
2. **Diagnosis.** An exact best response beats the J,K cycle by KO in round 1,
   so that failure came from a weak counter search, not from the rules. The
   ablation gaps were ceiling effects: both planner variants beat the weak
   fixed-style pool almost every time.
3. **Instruments added.** These were developed on `train/...` seeds:
   - a known-policy best response (beam search);
   - the history-reading `reader-v1` planner family;
   - predictable but competent scripted opponents for the ablations.
   No rule changed; the ruleset digest is unchanged.
4. **Held-out rerun.** Those instruments were designed after reading the
   first run's `test/...` results, so the gates were rerun on a fresh seed
   namespace, `holdout-20260923`. The table above comes from that run.

## Caveats that remain

- **Round-0 knockouts are 0% in the competent pool.** That is inside the
  ≤20% band, but it also means the first round never decides a competent
  fight.
- **Kick-heavy scripts are the strongest predictable style.** A fixed plan
  of mostly kicks beats scout-v1 and mixed-v1 about 90% of the time, and
  only history reading answers it (reader-v1 ≈ 0.75–0.9). Watch this style
  in a live metagame.
- **mixed-v1 and repeat-last-winner score below 0.45 against the pool.** The
  families gate passes on the other three.
- **Not measured:**
  - adaptation time against a style-switching opponent;
  - human replay readability (model.md §3);
  - latency and contract cost (§4; the C++ port reports work counters and a
    1,370 KiB state, not Core execution fees);
  - economics with real costs. [economics-report.md](economics-report.md)
    measures rake flows, skill transfer and ring farming on the reference
    contract with fake QU. It leaves out execution, hosting and provider
    costs, and it found that the per-pair cap bounds a small population to
    N(N-1) ranked fights per epoch.
