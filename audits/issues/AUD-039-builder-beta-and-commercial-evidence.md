# AUD-039 — Run a small builder beta and separate engagement from simulated economics

- **Status:** Open (product and milestone proposal)
- **Priority:** P1 — product validation and development priorities
- **Type:** Product research / business evidence
- **Scope:** roadmap, product-decisions.md, beta plan, aggregate participation measurements

## Finding and impact

The review found a substantial game surrounded by markets, royalties, stakes,
seasons and ownership mechanics, but the observed population was entirely
house-run. Simulated fights and market turnover establish system behavior;
they cannot establish independent demand, repeat participation or willingness to pay.

The review proposes prioritizing a free builder cohort and measuring the
build → compete → study → revise loop before expanding the commercial surface.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Record the next milestone's audience and scope in the decision register:
  independent bot builders, with practice as their introduction.
- [ ] Plan and run a cohort of ten independent builders after the entry and
  onboarding blockers are resolved; separate house/test identities in reporting.
- [ ] Record whether each builder reaches a first completed arena fight, the elapsed
  time and assistance required. Evaluate the proposed ten-minute onboarding target
  with both successful attempts and drop-offs in the denominator.
- [ ] Measure second submissions/revisions, repeat participation one week later,
  and whether builders can explain and demonstrate an improvement.
- [ ] Define the measurement method before recruiting; a small manual cohort log is
  sufficient initially. Do not count automatic bot ticks as returning human visits.
- [ ] Publish aggregate results and reasons for drop-off, then decide the next
  development cycle from that evidence.
- [ ] Keep willingness to pay explicitly unvalidated until separately researched;
  free retention and simulated market activity are not revenue evidence.
- [ ] Record which market/royalty/collection expansions are deferred for this
  milestone and which deployment work continues.
- [ ] Keep real execution, hosting/provider costs and production asset/deployment
  readiness as explicit dependencies of any paid-launch decision.
